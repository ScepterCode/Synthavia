CONTRAST RULES

06 — THE HONESTY RULES

The system's
real constraint.

NO UNSIGNED NUMBER

A figure without an owner's sign-off is never sent to the browser. It renders as an em dash with its source and “audit in progress”.

NO COUNTDOWN WITHOUT A DATE

The countdown runs only when a published event has a start date in the future. Otherwise the page is a recap.

NO LOGO BEFORE INK

A partner appears only when its agreement is marked countersigned in the admin. Until then the grid keeps its empty state.

NO TEASER FOR UNWRITTEN WORK

Drafts and in-review posts have no public existence — not even a “coming soon” card.

08 — ACCESSIBILITY

FOCUS

Every interactive element takes a 2px cyan :focus-visible ring. The browser default is never left in place.

MOTION

prefers-reduced-motion collapses every animation and transition to 0.01ms.

STRUCTURE

A skip link, one <main>, labelled nav landmarks, and the mobile sheet marked inert while closed.

TARGETS

Controls stay at or above 34px on touch, and the mobile nav sheet sets its links at 29px.
